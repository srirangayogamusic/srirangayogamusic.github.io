About the Trainer

16 years of teaching, and more than 3000 students along the way

Hi, I'm V.S. Anil. At Sri Ranga Yoga & Music, I look at wellness as a whole journey: a strong, flexible body, a calm and focused mind, and a happy heart. I'm a YCB Certified Yoga Trainer recognised by the Ministry of AYUSH, Government of India, and I bring that same authentic, time-tested practice into every single class.

Over the past 16 years I've taught Hatha yoga, Ashtanga yoga, meditation, and pranayama to more than 3000 students. They've come from all walks of life: corporate teams, officers, homemakers, students, working professionals, seniors, and kids. Some came to lose weight, some to build immunity, some just to feel less stressed and more themselves. Whatever brings you here, there's a place for you.

  • Personal attention for every age and fitness level
  • Yoga for specific health concerns and everyday wellbeing
  • Carnatic vocal music for all ages, 3 years and above

Vocal Music Classes

Carnatic vocal music, taught with patience and care

Whether you're just starting out or want to go deeper, you'll learn Carnatic vocals step by step. Classes are open to all ages, 3 years and above. Light music, bhajans, sloka chanting, and the Bhagavad Gita are also taught whenever you'd like to learn them.

Beginner Carnatic Syllabus

  • 1 Sarali Varusalu
  • 2 Tara Sthayi
  • 3 Mandra Sthayi
  • 4 Janta Varusalu
  • 5 Daatu Varusalu
  • 6 Alankaralu

Taught in person, always

Some things a screen can never carry

In a real room I can see the catch in your shoulder, guide your spine with my own hands, and feel the moment your breath settles. A screen cannot do that, and a raga cannot live inside a video. This knowledge has always passed from person to person, and I would like to keep it that way.

Hands-on correction

Your posture is adjusted safely, in real time, so you never quietly practise an asana the wrong way.

The energy of a shared room

Practising beside others lifts you in a way no screen ever will. You feed off the calm in the room.

A teacher who truly sees you

I notice your breath, your mood and your progress, and I meet you exactly where you are that day.

An unbroken tradition

Yoga and Carnatic music have always passed from guru to student, in person. That lineage matters.
