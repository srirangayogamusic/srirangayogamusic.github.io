About the Trainer

16 years of teaching, and more than 3000 students along the way

Hi, I'm V.S. Anil. At Sri Ranga Yoga & Music, I look at wellness as a whole journey: a strong, flexible body, a calm and focused mind, and a happy heart. I'm a YCB Certified Yoga Trainer recognised by the Ministry of AYUSH, Government of India, and I bring that same authentic, time-tested practice into every single class.

Over the past 16 years I've taught Hatha yoga, Ashtanga yoga, meditation, and pranayama to more than 3000 students. They've come from all walks of life: corporate teams, officers, homemakers, students, working professionals, seniors, and kids. Some came to lose weight, some to build immunity, some just to feel less stressed and more themselves. Whatever brings you here, there's a place for you.

🤝 Taught in person, always

Some things a screen can never carry

When you step onto the mat in a real room, something quietly changes. I can see the small catch in your shoulder, gently guide your spine with my own hands, and feel the very moment your breath finally settles. A camera cannot do that. It cannot catch the posture that might one day hurt you, and it cannot notice the day you walk in carrying something heavy on your heart.

Music is no different. A raga is not a row of notes on a video. It lives in the air between teacher and student, in the way a voice fills a room, in a correction offered the very instant it is needed. For thousands of years this knowledge has passed from person to person, breath to breath, presence to presence. I would like to keep it that way.

🖐️

Hands-on correction

🔥

🪔

An unbroken tradition

Yoga and Carnatic music have always passed from guru to student, in person. That lineage matters.

When you come to class, you are not logging in. You are showing up. You leave your phone at the door, you breathe the same air as the people beside you, and for one hour the world goes quiet. That hour is yours. That is why every class here is, and always will be, in person.
