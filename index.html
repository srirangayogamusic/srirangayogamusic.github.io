About the Trainer

Guiding 3000+ students to health & harmony for over 16 years

At Sri Ranga Yoga & Music, wellness is taught as a complete journey — a strong, flexible body, a calm and focused mind, and a joyful spirit. As a YCB Certified Yoga Trainer recognised by the Ministry of AYUSH, Government of India, V.S. Anil brings authentic, time-tested practice to every class.

With over 16 years of experience teaching Hatha yoga, Ashtanga yoga, meditation and pranayama, Anil has guided more than 3000 students — corporates, officers, homemakers, students, working professionals, seniors and kids — toward better health, weight loss, stronger immunity, stress relief and overall fitness.

  • ✔ Carnatic vocal music for all ages 3 & above
